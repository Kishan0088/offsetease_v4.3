Methodology

A five-stage cycle that compounds.

1

Assess

Defensible baseline. Full footprints measured, exposure mapped across jurisdictions, peers benchmarked, and the financial cost of inaction quantified.

2

Strategise

Funded pathway. Marginal abatement modelled and sequenced for finance-ability — ambitious enough to be credible, feasible enough to be funded.

3

Implement

Systems, data, delivery. Project structuring, technology procurement, supplier engagement, financing, and the data systems that make next year's disclosure cheaper.

4

Measure

Audit-ready performance. Instrumented against milestones in the language investors and auditors recognise — limited or reasonable assurance.

5

Improve

Compounding progress. Findings closed, ambition raised where affordable, architecture extended to absorb the next regulatory move at lower marginal cost.
