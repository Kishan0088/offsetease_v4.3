Methodology

The five-stage cycle.

A structured engagement model that compounds — each turn cheaper and more capable than the last.

1

Assess

We measure full footprints, map exposure across jurisdictions, benchmark peers on the methodologies you'll be judged by, and quantify the financial cost of inaction.

2

Strategise

Marginal abatement modelled and sequenced for finance-ability. Targets calibrated to be ambitious enough to be credible, feasible enough to be funded.

3

Implement

Project structuring, technology procurement, supplier engagement, financing — and the data systems that make next year's disclosure cheaper than this year's.

4

Measure

Instrumentation against milestones in the language investors and auditors recognise — limited or reasonable assurance.

5

Improve

Findings closed, ambition raised where it becomes affordable, architecture extended to absorb the next regulatory move at lower marginal cost.
